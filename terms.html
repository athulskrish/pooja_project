3. Tripartite Marketplace Role

Zippy operates as an electronic e-commerce marketplace aggregator connecting:

  • Customers: Users seeking delicious meals from localized outlets.
  • Restaurant Partners: Independent culinary businesses responsible for food preparation, recipe hygiene, and portion quantities.
  • Delivery Partners: Independent logistics contractors responsible for dispatching packaged parcels safely to the designated drop location.

4. Pricing, GST & Payments

All listed menu item prices are determined directly by the participating restaurant partners. Prices reflect inclusive or exclusive Goods and Services Tax (GST @ 5% on restaurant food services) as mandated by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).

Payments can be executed through certified third-party payment switches including Unified Payments Interface (UPI - Google Pay, PhonePe), Debit/Credit Cards, or Cash on Delivery (COD).

5. Order Cancellation & Refund Policy

Due to the perishable nature of prepared foodstuffs:

  • Grace Window: Orders can be cancelled within 60 seconds of submission for a 100% unconditional refund.
  • Kitchen Commenced: After the restaurant marks the ticket as accepted or in-cooking on the Kitchen Display System (KDS), cancellations will incur a 100% cancellation fee.
  • Refund Turnaround: Reversals for authorized cancellations are refunded to the original payment source within 2 hours for UPI and 3-5 banking days for debit/credit cards.

6. Delivery & Delivery Executive Handover

Delivery schedules and ETAs are estimations computed dynamically using our multi-factor routing heuristic algorithm. Delivery partners require a secret 4-digit Delivery OTP upon arrival to verify genuine parcel handover. Customers must ensure an authorized individual is present at the provided address to receive the parcel.

7. Food Safety, Hygiene & Allergens

All restaurant partners are required to hold valid Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) licenses. While Zippy mandates tamper-evident packaging, dietary allergen cross-contamination warnings (nuts, dairy, gluten, shellfish) remain the direct responsibility of the cooking kitchen.
