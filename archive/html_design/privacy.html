Privacy & Data Protection Policy

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate seamless food ordering and courier dispatch, we collect:

  • User Profile Data: Full name, phone number, email address, encrypted login passwords.
  • Delivery Coordinates: Precise GPS latitude/longitude, delivery address line, landmark, and postal pincode.
  • Transactional History: Items ordered, payment mode, invoice amounts, discount coupons applied.
  • Telemetry & Diagnostics: Browser user agent, device model, and IP address for fraud detection.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: Transmitting ticket items to the restaurant Kitchen Display System (KDS).
  • Algorithmic Dispatch: Computing optimal travel routes and live countdown ETAs for delivery executives.
  • Smart Recommendation (Apriori Mining): Analyzing basket items to suggest complementary sides and beverages.
  • Customer Support: Assisting in refund disputes and order status investigations.

3. Sharing with Restaurants & Logistics Partners

We respect data minimization principles. We only share essential delivery information:

• Kitchen Staff: See order item quantities, dish customizations, and first name only.
• Delivery Partner: Receives customer address and an in-app masked telephone proxy number to safeguard personal contact numbers.

We never sell or lease customer browsing datasets to third-party telemarketers or external advertising exchanges.

4. Enterprise Cryptography & Database Security

As documented in the MCA Project Technical Specifications (MCSP-232):

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plain text. Hashed using modern Argon2id with memory hardness.
  • SQL Injection Immunity: All database queries are 100% parameterized using PHP Data Objects (PDO) prepared statements.
  • Data in Transit: Encrypted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with modern cipher suites.
  • Payment Security: Card details are handled solely by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment switches.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use client-side localStorage to maintain your active food cart items (pooja_zippy_cart) and active coupon discount codes across page reloads without requiring server round-trips.

6. Your Data Rights & Erasure

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), you hold the right to:

  • Access a portable summary of all past orders and invoices.
  • Update or correct outdated address and profile information.
  • Request full account deletion and personal identifier erasure from our active database.
