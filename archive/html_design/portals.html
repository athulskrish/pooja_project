MCA Viva Voce Demonstration Strategy

How to Demonstrate Multi-Role Portals in Your Viva:

Open each of the four buttons in separate browser tabs side-by-side:
1. In Tab 1 (Customer): Add Biryani to cart and click "Pay & Place Order".
2. In Tab 2 (Kitchen KDS): Watch the ticket appear instantly sorted by WSJF score, then click "Order Ready".
3. In Tab 3 (Delivery App): Accept dispatch, enter the customer OTP, and click "Confirm Delivery".
4. In Tab 4 (Admin Dashboard): Observe the revenue metrics, audit trail, and SES demand charts update!
